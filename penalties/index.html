Penalties: Football League

Every penalty is two decisions: where to put it, and how hard to hit it.

Take the kick

Tap the goal to aim, then tap again to set the power. Power decides height: only a firm strike lifts the ball up under the bar, and a soft one stays low wherever you aimed. Hit it too hard and it goes wide of the post.

Make the save

You go in goal every other kick. Watch the striker's run-up — it leans towards the corner they have picked, though a good one will sell you a dummy. Tap the third you want to cover, and your keeper commits the moment the ball is struck.

Climb the pyramid

  • Six divisions, five rivals in each, a full table with points, wins, draws and losses
  • Every rival brings a different keeper, from a rookie who guesses late to a legend who reads you
  • From the fourth division up, picking the right side stops being enough: you have to call high or low as well
  • Finish first and you go up. Your season is waiting where you left it.

Also inside

  • Quick match against any class of keeper, with no season attached
  • Training: drill kicking alone or saving alone, for as long as you like
  • A guided first match that teaches by playing, and a How to Play screen you can open at any time
  • 33 languages
  • No ads between matches, no energy bar, no timer telling you when to come back

Free kicks are luck. Penalties are nerve.
