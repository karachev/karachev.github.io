How it plays

Both ends of the shootout

You do not just take penalties. Every other kick you are the one in goal, reading someone else's run-up.

1

Aim

Tap the goal to pick your corner. Left, middle or right — and from the fourth division up, high or low as well.

2

Strike

Tap again to stop the power bar. Power is height: only a firm strike lifts the ball under the bar, and too much sends it wide of the post.

3

Save

Now you are in goal. The run-up leans towards the corner they picked — but a good striker will sell you a dummy.
